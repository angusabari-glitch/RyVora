# Business logic boundary

Independent business calculations and source-row-to-domain mapping belong here and must consume validated workbook projections. Dashboard, Claims, Denials, Payments, AR, Exceptions, Interoperability, Analytics, Automation, and AI/HITL logic is module-scoped. Analytics reuses existing module summaries and scopes related records through Claim_ID. Automation reuses existing AR, denial, exception, payment, and 837 eligibility classifications; its workflow definitions are simulated and eligible populations are not executions. AI/HITL reuses Denials' linked AI recommendation and Human_Reviews records, preserving distinct source AI status and linked-review status. Calculations and selectors have focused automated tests. Keep rendering in pages and workbook loading in the data/service boundaries.
